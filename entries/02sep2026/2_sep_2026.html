They felt super easy and I was able to finish them in less than a pomodoro length!

After that, I got to a new section, where the cascading was explained. It was basically a dump of a lot of rules which determine which styles are applied in what order and how ties and conflicting styles are resolved.

The main takeaway I get from that lesson is that the order for style application is based on specificity, so ranking selectors this would mean conflicts and styles are applied in the following order:

  1. Ids
  2. Classes
  3. Elements

Furthermore, the more specificity is applied, the more likely that style is chosen over others for rendering.
