Some stuff I learned today

So, continuing from yesterday, today I will also be studying about the box model for CSS.

For the box model, I think this picture, sourced from the mdn doc for it, shows pretty well the gist of it or at least more efficiently than a bunch of words would.

As said before in yesterday's entry, the box's width and height define those attributes of the content of the box. Therefore, any padding and borders are added to those dimensions to define the total size taken by the box.

Turns out there is also an alternative CSS box model. This alternative box model is such that the width and height define the size. Therefore, to get the total size of the box there is no need to add the padding and the border to the defined width and height accordingly.

The margin pushes all of the boxes away from the boundary keeping the content, the border and the padding. If the margin is set to a negative value, then boxes can overlap with other things in the page. The margin is relative to the size of the visible box.

The margins can interact in different ways depending on the values.

  • Two positive margins combine into a single margin.
  • Two negative margins collaps and the smallest is kept.
  • Two different signed margins are collapsed so that the negative margin is substracted from the total.

Although the text uses the word "substract", I think it's more accurate to say that in this case too the margins are added, or at least that's what I got from the example presented.
